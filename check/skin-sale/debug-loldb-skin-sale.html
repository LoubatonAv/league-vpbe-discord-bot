Frequently Asked Questions

What is League of Legends Skin Sale?

League of Legends skin sales are rotating weekly promotions that offer a selection of skins at reduced prices. These discounts range from 25% to 60% off the original price, giving players a chance to save Riot Points on cosmetics they've been eyeing.

The sale rotation changes every week, offering fresh deals on different skins. This makes it worth checking the shop regularly to see if any of your desired skins are on sale before purchasing them at full price.

What Skins Go on Sale and What Don't?

Every week, certain skins go on sale, but not every skin is eligible. Here's what you can expect:

What to Expect:

  • Legacy Skins
  • Regular Skins
  • Epic Skins
  • Legendary Skins

What Not to Expect:

  • Skins released within the last 3 months
  • Mythic Skins
  • Ultimate Skins
  • Exalted Skins
  • Transcendent Skins
  • Exclusive skins (PAX Twisted Fate, Black Alistar, etc.)

The shop rotation focuses on standard tier skins that have been available for at least 3 months. Premium-tier skins like Ultimate, Mythic, and special exclusive releases never go on sale.

What is the Record Low Skin Price from a Sale?

The cheapest skin after a discount is Golden Alistar. It was priced at 175 RP during the sale on August 11, 2025, making it the lowest-priced skin available through the shop discount system.

When do Skin Sale Discounts Refresh?

Shop discounts refresh every Monday at 6:00 PM UTC. This is when the previous week's sale ends and a new rotation of 20 discounted items becomes available in the store.

When is My Favorite Skin Going on Sale?

Unfortunately, there's no way to predict when a specific skin will go on sale. Riot doesn't publish any schedule or information about upcoming sales. If a skin was recently discounted, it'll likely take several months or longer before it appears in the shop rotation again. You can add your favorite skins to your watchlist to get notifications whenever they go on sale.
