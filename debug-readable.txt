This page documents all confirmed changes and additions to cosmetics & gameplay found on the PBE server for League of Legends. Information added here is sourced from the game directly or must otherwise reference official Riot accounts.

Documented changes may or may not be final—cancelled content from the current PBE cycle should be stricken-through, or moved to the Upcoming section if confirmed/likely to return at a later patch. PBE content is finalized on the Friday before a new Live patch releases, and no later than the Monday at 12 PM PST before a new Live patch releases on rare occasions. At that point, the patch and its content has been locked and will be pushed to all Live regions on the day of the new patch cycle based on Riot's patch schedule. A new PBE cycle usually starts on the Tuesday at exactly 12 PM PST before a new Live patch releases.

;Matchmaking

* New matchmaking algorithm, TrueSkill2.

* It has been tested in Normal game queues, since February in the NA shard and since July in the KR shard.

* The test has been deemed successful and the algorithm is likely being deployed later in more shards and queues.

* After their first 10 required Normal games, smurfs are placed close to their appropriate skill level in about 5 Ranked games. This is 4 times faster than the previous system.
